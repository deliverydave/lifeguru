# Revoke abstract — honest limitations

When you revoke an abstract signal:

- It is excluded from future scoring and packs.
- Hypotheses may be recalculated without it.
- Packs already delivered to a private inbox are not retro-deleted (that would itself signal revocation).
- Influence already exerted on past coaching cannot be unlearned from your partners mind.
- Shared explicit artifacts you revoke are removed from the shared UI per policy.
