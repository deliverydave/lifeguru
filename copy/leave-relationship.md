# What happens when you leave

- Your private sessions, summaries, and memories stay with you. Your partner cannot see them.
- Anything you explicitly shared with your partner becomes read-only for them for 30 days, then is deleted from the shared space. They can copy it into their own private notes before then if they want to keep it.
- Abstract signals you allowed for relationship coaching are turned off immediately and will not influence new suggestions.
- Joint goals freeze and are archived; neither of you can edit them after leave.
- We cannot erase what your partner already read or practiced from a past shared card or coaching suggestion.
