# Privacy

## Stance

Two private coaching products linked by a narrow consent-gated relationship plane.

## Defaults

- Share decision default: Keep
- Raw transcript retention: 14 days hybrid then hard-delete
- Abstracts activate on nightly batch only
- No auto-abstract
- No joint sessions in V1

## Never

- Counselor A must never receive Partner B private data
- Free-text abstracts in RelInt
- Partner notify on safety
- Presence or in-session indicators
- Attribution in packs or themes
